1.0 Primary Structure

The primary structure is the linear sequence of amino acids in a polypeptide chain, linked together by covalent peptide bonds. It is determined directly by the mRNA sequence transcribed from DNA. The sequence determines how the protein folds, as the thermodynamic properties of the side chains dictate secondary and tertiary interactions.

Covalent Peptide bonds (CO-NH) define the backbone chain.
