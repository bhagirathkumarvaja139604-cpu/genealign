Reconstruct evolutionary lineages. Enter 3 to 6 ancestral sequences to calculate a pairwise distance matrix and compile a taxonomic hierarchical guide tree using the UPGMA clustering algorithm.

6.1Methodology Context

A phylogenetic tree is a diagrammatic representation of the evolutionary relationships and lines of descent among various biological species or genes.

The fundamental premise of molecular phylogenetics is that sequences accumulate mutations over time. Therefore, the number of differences (mutations) between two homologous sequences corresponds to their evolutionary distance.

Our pipeline calculates taxonomic ties through a two-step process:

  • Pairwise Distance Matrix: Computes Levenshtein edit distance between all pairs. It handles insertion/deletion (indel) events.
  • UPGMA Clustering: Group coordinates iteratively based on nearest distances, forming new nodes with heights proportional to half of their merge distance.
