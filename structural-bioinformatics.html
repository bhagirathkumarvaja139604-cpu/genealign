7.1Macromolecular Modeling

Understanding a protein's 3D structure is key to understanding its function, binding ligands, and engineering new molecules.

The Protein Data Bank (PDB) is the worldwide database for structural data on biological macromolecules, determined by experimental techniques such as X-ray crystallography, Nuclear Magnetic Resonance (NMR) spectroscopy, and Cryogenic Electron Microscopy (Cryo-EM).

Where experimental coordinates are unavailable, structure prediction algorithms must be used:

  • Homology Modeling: Builds 3D coordinates based on an aligned protein structure of a homologous sequence (template).
  • De Novo Modeling & AlphaFold: Deep learning models (like DeepMind's AlphaFold) predict native structure direct from primary sequence by learning residue spatial orientations and evolutionary constraints.
Note: This portal describes prediction methodologies conceptually; calculations are executed on remote CPU cluster backends, not client-side.
